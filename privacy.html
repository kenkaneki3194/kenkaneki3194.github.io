Sonora Privacy Policy

Last updated: [23,Nov,2026]

Sonora ("the app") is an Android music player and library organizer. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Summary

  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • The developer does not run a server for the app and does not receive your data.
  • Signing in with Google is optional.

Google user data the app accesses

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app accesses:

  • Your basic profile information (display name, email address and profile picture). This is read on your device to show your account in the app. A copy of the name, email and picture is stored only on your device and is removed when you sign out.
  • The app's hidden application data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app uses it only to store and retrieve backups of its own data. The app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

How the app uses this data

Only to show your account in the app, and to back up and restore the app's own data: your playlists, favorites, play history and counts, matched song information, and app settings. Backups never include API keys or passwords, and never include your music files.

Storage and sharing

Backups are stored in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only this app can open. The developer cannot access them. The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other features

These features are optional and work only when you use them. If you use song identification, an audio fingerprint or a short audio clip of the song you chose may be sent to the song recognition service used by the app, to find its title and artist. If you use the assistant, the text of your command is sent to the AI service you have configured. These services are not given your Google account information.

Your choices

  • Sign out or disconnect Google in the app at any time.
  • Delete your backups with "Delete my cloud data" in the app, or in your Google Drive settings under Manage apps.
  • Revoke the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear the app's data in your phone's settings to remove everything stored on the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
