Sonora

A music player and library organizer for Android

Sonora plays the music stored on your phone and helps you keep it tidy. It can identify songs from their audio, fix missing artist names, rename files with a naming pattern you choose, and manage playlists and favorites. An optional assistant can create and play playlists from plain-language commands.

Optional Google account features

You can optionally sign in with Google. Sonora uses your Google account to show your name and profile picture in the app, and to back up your playlists, favorites and settings to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore them after reinstalling or on a new phone. The app works fully without signing in.

Sonora shows no ads, includes no analytics or trackers, and has no server that stores your data.
